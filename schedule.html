Effective May 13, 2026

By scheduling your call with Jordan, APRN, FNP, you are agreeing to the following.

1. What you are consenting to

A 15-minute pre-op phone consult with Jordan, APRN, FNP to evaluate your fitness for anesthesia for an upcoming procedure. This is a clinical screening — not a substitute for in-person primary care.

2. Use and sharing of your health information

You authorize Atlas Anesthesia to use and share your protected health information so that we can:

  • Conduct this consult and provide anesthesia services on the day of your surgery
  • Coordinate with your dentist or oral surgeon, the surgery center, and (if you have one) your primary care physician
  • Process payment and securely store your card for the remaining anesthesia charges
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders by phone, text, and email

3. Service providers Atlas uses

Atlas works with these HIPAA-compliant vendors. Each has a signed Business Associate Agreement protecting your information:

4. Electronic communication

You consent to receive calls, texts, and emails from Atlas Anesthesia about your appointment and care, including the call from Jordan, APRN, FNP at the time you select.

5. Notice of Privacy Practices

You acknowledge that you have received and reviewed Atlas Anesthesia's Notice of Privacy Practices, which explains how your health information is used and disclosed and your rights regarding it.

Your rights

  • Request a copy of your records by emailing admin@atlasanesthesia.co
  • Ask Atlas to communicate with you only by a specific method (phone, email, etc.)
  • Revoke this authorization in writing at any time (this will not affect care already provided)
  • File a complaint with Atlas or with HHS Office for Civil Rights — Atlas will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint

Limits of the consult

  • This is a clinical screening for anesthesia fitness only
  • Final anesthesia clearance is determined the day of surgery based on your condition at that time

  • The $100 pre-op fee covers the screening and any related clinical review; it is non-refundable once the consult has taken place

Informed Consent for Anesthesia Care

Please read the entire document before agreeing.

1. Anesthesia care you are consenting to

I authorize any qualified contractor or subcontractor working for Atlas Anesthesia LLC to administer anesthesia for my procedure. I understand that depending on my medical history, the type of procedure, and conditions at the time of surgery, the anesthesia plan may include one or more of the following:

  • General anesthesia — medications that put me into a state of unconsciousness so I do not feel or remember the procedure
  • Monitored anesthesia care (MAC) / IV sedation — medications that make me very sleepy and comfortable while I continue to breathe on my own

I understand the anesthesia provider will choose and may adjust the technique that they believe is safest for me based on their clinical judgment.

2. Common risks and side effects

I understand that even when anesthesia is performed correctly, the following common side effects can occur, most of which resolve on their own:

  • Nausea or vomiting after the procedure
  • Sore throat, hoarseness, or cough
  • Dry mouth, lip or tongue soreness
  • Headache, dizziness, drowsiness, or grogginess
  • Muscle aches or shivering
  • Bruising, soreness, or minor swelling at the IV site
  • Confusion or memory disturbance (especially in older patients)
  • Minor injury to lips, teeth, gums, dental restorations, or the tongue from airway management
  • Eye irritation or rare corneal abrasion
  • Temporary nerve numbness or tingling from positioning

3. Serious risks (less common, but possible)

I understand that anesthesia, like any medical procedure, carries the possibility of serious complications. These are rare but can occur even when standard of care is met. They include but are not limited to:

  • Allergic reactions to anesthetic drugs, including severe (anaphylactic) reactions
  • Difficult or failed intubation, dental damage from airway equipment, vocal cord injury
  • Aspiration of stomach contents into the lungs, which can cause pneumonia or breathing problems
  • Awareness or recall during general anesthesia
  • Damage to teeth, dentures, crowns, bridges, or veneers
  • Nerve injury, including weakness, numbness, or chronic pain
  • Cardiovascular events including irregular heart rhythms, heart attack, or cardiac arrest
  • Stroke or other neurologic injury, including (very rarely) brain damage
  • Malignant hyperthermia (a rare genetic reaction to some anesthetic agents)
  • Blood clots, bleeding, or infection
  • Postoperative cognitive dysfunction
  • In extremely rare cases, death

My personal risk depends on my age, medical history, the medications I take, and the type of surgery. I have had the opportunity to discuss my individual risk with the Atlas team during my pre-op clearance visit, and I will have a final opportunity to ask questions on the day of surgery before anesthesia begins.

4. Changes to the anesthesia plan

I authorize the Atlas anesthesia provider to change the type of anesthesia, the route of medication, or the airway management plan at any time during the procedure if, in their professional judgment, this is necessary for my safety or for the success of the procedure.

5. Emergency care and other necessary procedures

I authorize the Atlas anesthesia provider to perform any additional procedures or administer any medications they reasonably believe are necessary in an emergency, including but not limited to:

  • Placement of additional IV lines or airway devices
  • Administration of emergency medications
  • Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), defibrillation, or other resuscitation efforts
  • Calling 911 and transfer to a hospital for further care

If a blood transfusion is ever required in an emergency, I understand the provider will coordinate with the receiving facility; Atlas does not administer blood products in office-based settings.

6. Patient responsibilities

For my own safety, I agree to the following pre-operative responsibilities:

  • NPO / fasting: nothing to eat or drink (including water, gum, candy, mints, and tobacco) for at least 8 hours before my procedure unless told otherwise by Atlas
  • Medication disclosure: I have disclosed all prescription medications, over-the-counter medications, supplements, herbal products, and recreational substances I take, and any history of alcohol or drug use
  • Medical history disclosure: I have disclosed all known medical conditions, prior surgeries, allergies, family history of anesthesia complications, and bleeding disorders
  • Pregnancy: if there is any chance I may be pregnant, I will disclose this before the procedure
  • Responsible adult driver: I will arrange for a responsible adult to drive me home and stay with me for several hours after the procedure
  • No driving, work, or signing legal documents for several hours after anesthesia

I understand that failing to follow these instructions can delay or cancel my procedure and can put my safety at serious risk.

7. No guarantee of outcome

I understand that medicine is not an exact science and that no guarantees or assurances have been made to me about the results of my anesthesia care.

  • I have read and understood this entire document
  • I have had the opportunity to ask questions and to discuss my anesthesia care with the Atlas team
  • I am voluntarily consenting to anesthesia care as described above
  • I am at least 18 years old, or am the legal guardian / parent of a minor patient and am signing on their behalf
